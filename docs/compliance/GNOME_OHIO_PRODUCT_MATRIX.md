# Gnome Ohio Product Matrix

Checked: 2026-08-24
Jurisdiction: Ohio / Northeast Ohio beta
Status: Working product matrix for counsel/regulatory review. Not legal advice.

## Launch Verdict

Ohio can support a constrained beta if Gnome keeps ordinary produce low-friction and fails closed on high-risk regulated categories. The matrix below should be loaded as policy guidance only after Daniel/counsel approves exact wording and category mappings.

## Simple Seller Questionnaires

### Eggs

1. Are these eggs from your own birds?
2. Approximately how many laying birds do you keep?
3. Where will buyers pick up the eggs?
4. Are the eggs washed, graded, packaged, and kept refrigerated?
5. Do you have an egg or local health/MRFE license if one applies?

### Baked Goods

1. Did you make this yourself?
2. Does it need refrigeration?
3. Does it contain cream, custard, meat, cheese, or another perishable filling?
4. Is it individually packaged and labeled?

### Honey

1. Is this honey produced from your own hives?
2. Is it plain honey or infused/flavored?
3. What size container and label will you use?

### Seeds

1. Is this an unopened commercial seed packet?
2. Did you package or label the seed yourself?
3. What crop/species/variety is listed on the label?
