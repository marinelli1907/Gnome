# Gnome Ohio Launch Compliance

Checked: 2026-08-24
Jurisdiction: Ohio first.
Status: Pre-launch guidance for Boone Systems review. Not legal advice.

## Ohio Launch Verdict

**Ohio is not fully ready for unrestricted category launch.** It is reasonable to launch a restricted Ohio beta if Gnome allows low-risk categories, requires seller attestations/labels where appropriate, verifies credentials where required, and blocks high-risk categories pending attorney/ODA/local health review.

## Mandatory Seller Fields for Ohio Beta

For every food listing:

- Producer/seller identity.
- Product category and exact item.
- Ingredients where multi-ingredient.
- Major allergens where applicable.
- Storage/refrigeration instructions where applicable.
- Pickup/delivery method.
- Attestation that seller has legal authority to sell.

For regulated categories:

- Credential type.
- Issuing agency.
- Credential number.
- Issue/expiration date.
- Document upload.
- Scope/category covered.
- Review status and reviewer notes.

## Ohio Launch Category Recommendation

Launch allowed:

- Whole raw produce.
- Fresh herbs.
- Whole fruit.
- Raw/comb/creamed honey.
- Maple syrup.
- Non-TCS cottage baked goods.
- Jams/jellies/preserves that fit cottage food.
- Cultivated mushrooms only if not wild/foraged.
- Firewood local-only.

Launch allowed with credential/review:

- Eggs beyond narrow on-farm/direct scenario.
- Home bakery refrigerated/TCS baked goods.
- Meat from inspected source.
- Nursery stock/live plants.
- Self-labeled seeds.

Launch blocked:

- Raw milk for human consumption.
- Wild mushrooms/foraged food.
- Pickles/salsa/acidified canned goods without verified licensed process.
- Prepared meals/frozen meals.
- Seafood/shellfish.
- Pet food/treats/feed without registration.
- Bait pending ODNR/ODA review.
