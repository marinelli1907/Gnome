## Questions for Counsel

1. Does Gnome's off-platform payment model avoid, reduce, or fail to change marketplace facilitator tax treatment in Ohio?
2. Is the independent-seller notice strong enough without overstating waivers of consumer rights?
3. Which Ohio categories should Gnome block until professional review?
4. What seller representations and indemnities are enforceable and appropriate?
5. Should Gnome require 18+ for all accounts at launch, or only seller accounts?
6. What retention period should Gnome use for AI chats, credentials, moderation evidence, and account deletion records?
7. Is Zordy's horticulture diagnostic feature medical/chemical/pesticide-risk sensitive enough to require stronger disclaimers?
