## Top 10 Gnome Advantages

1. Seller-first AI listing/photo workflow.
2. Zordy horticulture/garden assistant.
3. Compliance credential center and server-side publish gates.
4. Off-platform payment model keeps seller payment relationship and can avoid payment custody.
5. No commission positioning.
6. Privacy-safe approximate location architecture.
7. Market storefronts plus QR/local stand use case.
8. Garden Planner creates non-commerce engagement.
9. Detailed taxonomy for local food/homestead goods.
10. Founder-led local launch can build trust personally.

## Top 10 Competitive Risks

1. Coop is already live on iOS and Android.
2. Coop appears to have integrated payments/order flow.
3. Coop is actively spreading through local Facebook/community posts.
4. Coop's app-store reviews create social proof.
5. Gnome's legal/compliance gates may slow seller onboarding.
6. Off-platform payments reduce checkout convenience.
7. Gnome has no proven launch inventory density yet.
8. Gnome must explain pricing while competitors may be free or transaction-based.
9. Broad taxonomy creates regulatory surface competitors may ignore.
10. AI differentiation only matters if it is reliable, cheap, and privacy-acceptable.

## Top 5 Things Competitors Do Better Today

1. Live app-store distribution and reviews.
2. Payment/checkout convenience.
3. Simpler seller promise.
4. Existing community awareness.
5. Less visible compliance friction.

## Top 5 Things Gnome Must Not Copy

1. Do not claim regulatory safety without verification.
2. Do not rush integrated payments before tax/insurance/legal review.
3. Do not hide seller responsibility behind platform convenience.
4. Do not broaden nationally without state compliance coverage.
5. Do not market features that are not actually implemented.

## Strategic Position

Gnome can win by being the trusted local-food operating system for small sellers, not by trying to out-generic-marketplace Coop. The pitch is: "List faster, sell locally, keep your payment relationship, protect your pickup privacy, and get help from Zordy."
