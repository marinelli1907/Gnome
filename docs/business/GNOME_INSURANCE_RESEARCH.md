# Gnome Insurance Research

Checked: 2026-08-24
Status: Broker research brief. Do not purchase coverage from this document alone.

## Marketplace-Specific Issue

Gnome does not manufacture food and currently does not process neighbor-to-neighbor payments. That helps but does not eliminate exposure. A claimant can still allege negligent platform design, negligent moderation, failure to remove dangerous listings, misleading trust/compliance messaging, privacy/security failures, or participation in a transaction. Product-liability allegations may still name Boone Systems even if independent sellers are primarily responsible.

## Coverage Recommendations

Must evaluate before launch:

- Commercial General Liability with products-completed operations endorsement/coverage.
- Technology Errors & Omissions.
- Cyber liability/privacy/data breach.
- Media liability/personal and advertising injury.
- Crime/social engineering, if funds/admin systems grow.

Should have soon:

- Commercial umbrella.
- Business Owner Policy if office/property exposure exists.
- D&O if outside investors/board appear.
- EPLI/workers comp when employees exist.

Later-stage:

- Higher cyber limits.
- Product liability tailored to food marketplace exposure.
- International/state expansion endorsements.
- Incident response retainer.

## Must-Have Before Launch

Recommended minimum before public beta:

- GL/products-completed operations, at least $1M occurrence/$2M aggregate if available.
- Tech E&O/cyber bundle or separate cyber/privacy policy.
- Written broker confirmation on marketplace/product-liability allegations involving independent food sellers.

## Broker Questions

1. Does the GL policy include products-completed operations for alleged injuries from products sold by independent platform sellers?
2. Are independent sellers excluded? If so, does that exclusion also bar defense for Boone Systems?
3. Does Tech E&O cover marketplace matching/reservation failures and software bugs?
4. Does cyber cover phone/email/location/pickup-address/seller-credential documents?
5. Are AI-generated listing suggestions excluded?
6. Are food, cottage food, eggs, meat, dairy, plants/nursery stock, pet food/feed, and bait excluded?
7. Does the policy cover regulatory investigation defense costs?
8. What security controls are underwriting requirements?
9. What are incident notification panel counsel/forensic vendor requirements?
10. Does coverage change if Gnome later processes payments?
