# Gnome Insurance and Broker Report

Checked: 2026-08-24
Status: Broker shortlist and coverage research. This is not an insurance quote or coverage opinion.

## Executive Recommendation

Before a public Northeast Ohio launch, Boone Systems should talk to an insurance broker about a combined startup/platform package:

- Commercial General Liability with products-completed operations defense.
- Technology Errors & Omissions.
- Cyber/privacy liability.
- Media liability or advertising injury coverage.
- Umbrella/excess once launch scope grows.
- D&O/EPLI/workers comp later, when investors, directors, employees, or contractors justify it.

The broker question to ask every time:

> If a customer becomes ill or injured from a product sold by an independent seller through Gnome and Boone Systems is named in the lawsuit, will this policy provide Boone Systems a legal defense and indemnity, and what exclusions apply?

Also ask whether product liability and Technology E&O leave any coverage gap between physical product injury, privacy breach, platform failure, AI advice, and seller misconduct.

## Coverage Questions for Broker

1. Does the CGL/products-completed operations policy defend Boone when an independent seller's food/product allegedly injures a buyer?
2. Are independent sellers treated as vendors, insureds, excluded parties, or unrelated third parties?
3. Is user-generated content, product labeling, allergen misinformation, or seller credential wording covered or excluded?
4. Does Tech E&O cover AI listing/plant diagnosis errors, reservation/inventory failures, and platform downtime?
5. Does cyber cover breach notification, regulatory defense, forensic investigation, business interruption, and third-party privacy claims?
6. Are there exclusions for marketplaces, food, agriculture, raw milk, cannabis/CBD, animals, live plants, seeds, delivery, or payment fraud?
7. What minimum seller insurance, indemnity, or rule language would underwriters expect?
8. Does coverage require MFA for admin accounts, backups, incident response, security scans, or vendor controls?
