## Reading the statuses

- **CLEARED** — explicit authority (scope exclusion, labeled-by-another
  exemption, small-package exemption, or an official no-licensing statement)
  covers an out-of-state reseller of pre-labeled sealed packets. Some CLEARED
  rows are **conditional on supplier credentials** (WI, IA — see below);
  the condition is part of the row, not a footnote to ignore.
- **REGISTRATION REQUIRED** — the state licenses sellers with no applicable
  exemption; the row lists the registration and cost. All are administrative
  (roughly $10–$150/yr each; FL/SC/AL scale with receipts).
- **AGENCY CONFIRMATION REQUIRED** — the law is ambiguous for THIS model or a
  controlling source could not be verified; a precise question and send-ready
  inquiry exist in the region file. **These states ship nothing until answered.**

## Cross-cutting findings the rollout must honor

1. **Supplier-credential dependency (the big one).** In WI, IA, MN, CT, WA,
   OR, TX, and CO — and for Ohio's own 907.08(H) seller liability — lawful
   resale depends on the SUPPLIER holding that state's labeler
   permit/registration. One supplier-credential audit unblocks many states at
   once; a supplier without a state's credential silently blocks that
   supplier's packets for that state (per-lot `allowed/excluded states` in the
   inventory model).
2. **Small-package exemptions carry the Mountain West** (MT ≤10 lb sealed,
   ID <8 oz, AZ <1 lb, NV ≤½ lb fee-exempt, WA ≤8 oz, OR ≤½ lb, CA ≤4 oz
   assessment-exempt) — packet weight/size must be tracked per SKU because
   these thresholds are load-bearing.
3. **Flower and unlisted-herb seed coverage varies state by state** (outside
   the acts in IN, IL, IA, MO, RI, CA…; fully covered in WI, MI, NE, ND, SD,
   MN…). Catalog classification must be per-state.
4. **Brassica packets may face WA (crucifer quarantine) and OR (blackleg)
   product-level restrictions** — unresolved; first candidates for per-product
   `excluded_states`.
5. **Germination-test currency binds SELLERS in several states** (OH 12/36 mo,
   RI 9 mo, CT 10 mo, PA/NY/NH 12-mo sell-by, ND 12/36) — inventory rotation
   and `retest/sell-by` tracking are compliance features, not nice-to-haves.
6. **Reporting cadence where registered:** semi-annual (IN, OK), quarterly
   (WV, AL), annual declarations (CA), staggered renewal dates — the admin
   command center's compliance calendar must carry these.
