## Inquiry 1 — USDA AMS SRTD

### Plain-text email version

> **To:** Ernest.Allen@ams.usda.gov
> **Cc:** todd.erickson@ams.usda.gov; seedcomplaints@usda.gov
> **Subject:** Federal Seed Act labeling questions — reseller of unopened, originally-labeled retail seed packets (Boone Systems LLC, Ohio)

Dear Director Allen,

I am writing to request written guidance on how the Federal Seed Act and
7 CFR Part 201 apply to a direct-to-consumer business model, before we begin
operating.

**The model, exactly.** Boone Systems LLC is an Ohio limited liability company
operating Gnome Farmers Market. We would purchase sealed retail seed packets at
wholesale from established seed companies and resell them, **unopened and
unaltered**, to home gardeners. We do not repackage, relabel, blend, treat,
condition, or open the packets. Each packet reaches the customer exactly as it
left the supplier, bearing the supplier's own label. We would group several
such packets into a themed shipment (4, 8, or 12 packets) in an outer mailer
with a packing slip, shipped by mail from Ohio to consumers in other states.

I would be grateful for your guidance on the following:

1. **Interstate shipper identification.** 7 CFR 201.27(c) identifies the
   interstate shipper of vegetable seed as the seller or consignor who puts the
   seed into interstate commerce. In this model that is Boone Systems LLC,
   whose name does not appear on the individual packets. Does the labeling
   requirement look to the entity that labeled the seed (our supplier), or to
   the entity that places it into interstate commerce (us)?

2. **Must our name appear on each packet?** If Boone Systems LLC must be
   identified, must that identification appear on **every individual packet**?

3. **May the outer container carry it instead?** May Boone Systems LLC be
   identified only on the outer mailer, shipping box, or enclosed packing slip
   rather than on each packet?

4. **Supplemental sticker.** Would a small supplemental Boone Systems LLC
   sticker applied to a packet be permissible if it covers, obscures, or alters
   **no** part of the supplier's existing label?

5. **Does that sticker make us a labeler?** If such a sticker is permitted,
   would applying it cause Boone Systems LLC to become a "labeler" for other
   purposes under the Act or Part 201?

6. **AMS code designation.** Is an AMS code designation available or
   appropriate for a direct-to-consumer reseller in this position, and would it
   satisfy the identification requirement?

7. **Does the supplier's identity suffice?** May the original seed company's
   name and address on the packet satisfy the interstate-shipper identification
   requirement without any additional identification from us?

8. **Drop-shipping.** Would any of the above answers change if the supplier
   shipped packets directly to our customer on our instruction, rather than our
   holding and shipping inventory ourselves?

9. **Records and file samples.** How do the three-year record-retention
   requirement and the file-sample requirement of 7 CFR 201.4 (a sample of at
   least 400 seeds) apply to a reseller of small finished retail packets that
   are never opened? Specifically, are we expected to open and sacrifice retail
   packets in order to retain file samples?

10. **Treatment, organic, advertising, and small packets.** How do the
    requirements for treated-seed labeling, organic representations,
    advertising, and any small-packet provisions apply to this exact model,
    where all such statements originate on the supplier's label?

11. **Seed kinds.** Do any of the answers above differ by seed kind — for
    vegetable seed, agricultural seed, herb seed listed in 7 CFR 201.2, herb
    seed *not* listed there, and flower seed?

Because we intend to build our compliance procedures around your answers, I
would greatly appreciate a **written response** (email is fine). I am happy to
provide any additional detail, sample packaging, or a description of our
records system that would be helpful.

Thank you for your time and for the Division's work.

Sincerely,
Daniel Marinelli
Boone Systems LLC — Gnome Farmers Market
[street address], Ohio [ZIP]
[phone] · [business email]

## Inquiry 2 — Ohio Department of Agriculture, Grain, Feed & Seed

### Plain-text email version

> **To:** GFS@agri.ohio.gov
> **Cc:** cholton@agri.ohio.gov
> **Subject:** Ohio seed law questions — reseller of unopened, originally-labeled seed packets (Boone Systems LLC, Ohio)

Dear Mr. Holton and the Grain, Feed and Seed program,

I am an Ohio business owner seeking written guidance before beginning
operations, so that we start in compliance rather than correct course later.

**The model, exactly.** Boone Systems LLC is an Ohio limited liability company
operating Gnome Farmers Market. We would buy sealed retail seed packets at
wholesale from established seed companies — each already bearing that company's
own label — and resell them **unopened and unaltered** to home gardeners. We do
not label, repackage, relabel, blend, treat, or condition seed. Several packets
would be grouped into a themed shipment in an outer mailer with a packing slip,
fulfilled from a location in Ohio. Our first phase would ship only within Ohio.

I would be grateful for written answers to the following:

1. **Labeler permit.** Does Boone Systems LLC need an Ohio Seed Labeler Permit
   under ORC Chapter 907 when it does not label seed and does not alter the
   supplier's packets in any way? My reading of ORC 907.13 is that the permit
   attaches to labeling rather than to selling, but I would like that confirmed
   rather than assumed.

2. **Other registrations.** Does any separate dealer, distributor, warehouse,
   or other registration apply to a business in this position?

3. **Fees and reports.** Would any inspection fees, annual reports, or sales or
   tonnage reports apply to us as a non-labeling seller — and if so, on what
   basis and on what schedule?

4. **Home-based fulfillment.** If order fulfillment occurs at a residential
   location in Ohio, must that location be registered, licensed, or inspected?

5. **Split locations.** Is it permissible to store inventory at one location
   (for example a climate-controlled storage unit used only for storage, with
   no customer traffic) and pack orders at a separate location?

6. **Records.** What records does the Department expect a non-labeling seller
   to keep, and for how long?

7. **Supplier permits.** ORC 907.08 places duties on sellers. Are we required
   to verify that each upstream labeler holds a current Ohio seed labeler
   permit before offering its packets for sale in Ohio, and if so, what
   verification does the Department consider adequate? Is the Department's
   online Seed Labeler Search the appropriate tool?

8. **Interstate shipments from Ohio.** Do any of the answers above change for
   orders shipped from our Ohio location to customers in other states?

9. **Germination-date limits.** How should we treat packets whose germination
   test date approaches or passes the statutory limit while in our inventory —
   specifically, are we permitted to sell them, and what is the Department's
   expectation for removing them from sale?

We would rather over-comply than guess, so any correction to the assumptions
above is welcome. A **written response** (email is fine) would let us build our
procedures around it and cite it in our records.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
Daniel Marinelli
Boone Systems LLC — Gnome Farmers Market
[street address], Ohio [ZIP]
[phone] · [business email]
