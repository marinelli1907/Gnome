# 07 — Supplier Review: Wholesale Sources for Sealed Retail Seed Packets

**Gnome Seed Drop V1 — Boone Systems LLC (Ohio). Drafted 2026-08-13.**

Scope: identify established DOMESTIC suppliers that plausibly meet Gnome's sourcing
requirements — finished, sealed retail seed packets sold at wholesale, complete
labeling with lot/test info, authorized commercial resale, recall/traceability
support, consistent replenishment of ordinary vegetable/herb/flower varieties,
usable inventory data, and (ideally) drop-ship capability. Gnome resells packets
UNOPENED and UNALTERED; the supplier's original label stays controlling.

> **Evidence standard.** Everything below is sourced from each supplier's OFFICIAL
> public pages (or their official ordering portals), accessed **2026-08-13** unless
> noted. Supplier websites establish **what they offer** — they establish **no legal
> conclusions**. Whether a given program permits Gnome's specific model (online,
> personalized, subscription resale from one Ohio location) must be confirmed **in
> writing** with each supplier before any purchase order. No supplier was contacted
> for this review.

> **Plain statement on resale rights.** Buying retail seed packets — at retail OR at
> wholesale — does **not** automatically grant (a) the right to resell them
> commercially under the supplier's program terms, (b) any right to use the
> supplier's trademarks, brand name, or packet artwork in Gnome's marketing or app
> UI, or (c) any right to reproduce packet photography or descriptions. Each of
> those requires written authorization. First-sale doctrine arguments are not a
> substitute for a signed reseller agreement; Gnome's model (curated subscription,
> supplier names surfaced in-app) needs explicit contractual cover.

## 2. Due-diligence / contract checklist (apply to EVERY supplier)

Get each item **in writing** (reseller agreement, program terms, or signed
addendum) before the first PO. "The website said so" is not a term.

| # | Item | What Gnome needs |
|---|------|------------------|
| 1 | **Authorized resale in writing** | Explicit authorization to resell sealed packets ONLINE via a personalized subscription from one Ohio location (not just a physical rack). |
| 2 | Minimum orders | Initial + reorder minimums, per-variety multiples, seasonal deadlines. |
| 3 | Wholesale pricing | Price list, tiers, discount off SRP, price-protection window, MAP policy if any. |
| 4 | Use of supplier name | Written license scope for naming the brand in-app, in marketing, and in drop contents lists. |
| 5 | Packet photography rights | Right to photograph packets and/or use supplier-provided product images (esp. artist-licensed packets, e.g. Hudson Valley). |
| 6 | Product description rights | Right to reproduce/adapt variety descriptions and growing instructions in the app. |
| 7 | Trademark use | Explicit trademark license or nominative-use guardrails, approval process, termination effects. |
| 8 | Inventory availability data | How Gnome learns what's in stock before promising a drop: feed, portal, rep, or none. |
| 9 | Lot / germination test data feed | Lot numbers, germ test dates/results, packed-for-season year — per shipment, in usable form. |
| 10 | Recall notification obligations | Supplier duty to notify Gnome of recalls/stop-sales; required speed; Gnome's downstream duty to customers. |
| 11 | Returns | Return-for-credit terms (e.g., Seed Savers up to 25%; Hart sale-and-return; Lake Valley buy-back option), deadlines, condition requirements. |
| 12 | Damaged packets | Claim window, evidence (photos), credit vs replacement. |
| 13 | Drop-shipping terms + territory | Whether supplier will ship direct to Gnome's customers; branding of that shipment; territory limits. |
| 14 | Data feeds / APIs | Any structured feed (inventory, catalog, images) and permitted use in Gnome's systems. |
| 15 | Substitutions | Whether supplier may substitute varieties on an order; Gnome needs veto or at least notice (personalization breaks on silent substitution). |
| 16 | Exclusivity | None expected V1 — confirm no exclusivity/non-compete is buried in program terms. |
| 17 | Supplier insurance | Product liability coverage; request **vendor endorsement / additional-insured** status for Boone Systems LLC; certificate of insurance annually. |
| 18 | Indemnification | Supplier indemnifies for seed defects, mislabeling, and IP claims arising from supplier content Gnome was licensed to use. |
| 19 | Compliance representations | Written rep that packets conform to the Federal Seed Act and applicable state seed laws as labeled, and that supplier holds required licenses; Gnome's Ohio-side obligations tracked separately in the compliance matrix. |
| 20 | Destination restrictions | Supplier flags state/territory shipping restrictions (noxious-weed lists, agricultural quarantines) at variety level so Gnome can block them at checkout. |
| 21 | Reseller qualification docs | What supplier requires from Gnome: Ohio vendor's license / sales tax ID (Seed Savers explicitly requires a state tax ID), resale certificate, business info. |
| 22 | Termination + sell-off | Right to sell remaining inventory after program termination; label/brand use during sell-off. |

## 3. Ranking by fit and first approaches

Fit = (a) sells finished sealed retail packets at wholesale, (b) documented program
with low/flexible minimums, (c) ordinary-variety depth + replenishment, (d)
plausibility of approving an online subscription reseller, (e) label/lot rigor.

| Rank | Supplier | Fit summary |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Botanical Interests** | Purpose-built retail packets, reorder-any-quantity claim (ideal for personalized drops), high availability claim, real wholesale infrastructure. Terms unpublished — must confirm online-reseller eligibility. |
| 2 | **Seed Savers Exchange** | Most transparent published terms of the group (250-packet initial, $1.75/packet option, ≤25% return credit, ≥50% off SRP, tax-ID requirement). Nonprofit heirloom story fits Gnome's brand. Rack-oriented terms need adaptation in writing. |
| 3 | **High Mowing Organic Seeds** | Published reseller program, $4.15 SRP anchor, two cost tiers, $200 free-freight threshold, sample-friendly. The certified-organic line is the obvious premium/organic uplift base. |
| 4 | **Lake Valley Seed** | Wholesale-to-independents is their entire business; buy-back OR outright terms; 750+ ordinary varieties incl. Value Packs. Application-driven terms. |
| 5 | **Chas. C. Hart Seed** (research find) | Sale-and-return with no upfront cash = lowest inventory risk of any documented program; 300+ varieties. Smaller brand cachet. |
| 6 | **Hudson Valley Seed Co** | $100 minimum / multiples of 6 = easiest documented entry, but premium Art Packs price high — best as the premium-uplift SKU, not the base. |
| 7 | **Livingston Seed** | Wholesale-only packet house, value pricing — but contact-driven terms, and the Ohio angle is gone (Norton, MA since the 2014 sale). |
| 8 | **Renee's Garden / Territorial** | Real programs, zero published terms; rep-managed. Worth a call in round 2. |
| — | Johnny's, Burpee, Ferry-Morse/GGP, Ohio Heirloom, NE Seed, Sow Right | Screened out for V1 (see §1.9). |

### Recommend approaching first (2–3)

1. **Botanical Interests** — the reorder-any-quantity Direct Ship model is the
   single best structural match for personalized 4–20-packet drops: Gnome can hold
   thin inventory and replenish per-variety. Priority questions: online-reseller
   eligibility, price list, minimums, image/name license, inventory visibility.
2. **Seed Savers Exchange** — published, predictable economics to model against
   ($1.75/packet with a return-credit valve caps downside on a 250-packet start),
   and the heirloom/nonprofit story is on-brand for Gnome's gardening identity.
   Priority questions: does the Seed Rack Program cover online subscription resale;
   lot/germ data per shipment; brand-use terms.
3. **High Mowing Organic Seeds** — locks in the organic premium tier and a second
   geographic/genetic source so one supplier outage can't kill a season. Priority
   questions: cost tiers, non-rack resale, per-variety ordering outside fixed
   collections.

**Backstop:** Hart Seed's sale-and-return should be quoted in parallel — if either
of the top two stalls on terms, Hart's zero-upfront risk profile is the cheapest
way to get V1 inventory breadth. Lake Valley is the volume play once demand is
proven.
