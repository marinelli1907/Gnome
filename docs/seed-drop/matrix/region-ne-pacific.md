# Seed Drop — Destination-State Regulatory Matrix: Northeast + Pacific (12 states)

**Prepared:** 2026-08-13 · **Entity:** Boone Systems LLC (Ohio) · **Scope:** ME, NH, VT, MA, RI, CT, NY, NJ, PA, WA, OR, CA

**Business model (locked):** Boone Systems LLC, an Ohio company, resells UNOPENED, UNALTERED retail seed packets (ordinary vegetable, herb, flower) purchased wholesale from established domestic suppliers. The supplier's original label stays intact and controlling. Several packets are combined into a plain padded envelope/box with a Gnome packing slip, sold as a personalized subscription, shipped from Ohio direct to consumers in the destination state. No live plants, bulbs, soil, imported seed, hemp/cannabis, noxious weeds, or quarantined/recalled products.

**Evidence standard:** Authority = state statutes, administrative codes (official legislature/state code sites), the state agriculture department's official seed-program pages, official applications and fee schedules. Each material conclusion carries a citation, direct URL, access date (2026-08-13), the shortest controlling quotation, and an EXPLICIT / INTERPRETATION label. No agency has been contacted; all inquiries below are DRAFTS ONLY.

**Cross-cutting structural finding (applies to most states below):** These are RUSSL-family laws. Where a license/registration exists, it almost always attaches to the **person who LABELS the seed or whose name appears on the label**, not to a downstream reseller of factory-sealed packets. The recurring hinge points are: (1) whether "label"/"labeling" is defined to include material *accompanying* the seed (which would sweep in Gnome's packing slip — Pennsylvania is the clearest example); (2) whether a small-package or labeled-by-a-licensee exemption exists (WA, OR, NH have explicit ones); and (3) whether the *supplier* holds that state's labeler credential, since several states condition lawful resale on the seed having been labeled by a registrant (CT, WA, OR). Supplier-credential verification (which suppliers hold CT/WA/OR/ME/NH/VT credentials) is the single highest-leverage compliance action for this region.

- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** **Yes.** "Every labeler of agricultural or vegetable seed offered for sale in this state, or any person, as defined in Section 52256.5, who sells that seed in this state, shall annually register with the secretary to obtain authorization to sell agricultural or vegetable seed before engaging in this activity…" (§52351; exceptions: individual growers conditioning own seed, seed-increase planters, licensed nursery-stock sellers). [EXPLICIT] CDFA's application instructions phrase it: "every labeler of agricultural and/or vegetable seed offered for sale or sold in this state, or any person who receives or possesses for sale or sells in this state seed not grown in this state shall annually register." [EXPLICIT agency text] An out-of-state company selling vegetable/herb seed to California consumers falls within "any person … who sells that seed in this state." [INTERPRETATION — the standard reading; CDFA's form contemplates out-of-state firms ("CALIFORNIA FIRMS ONLY" location field)]
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** Registration is the single credential; "Labeler" = "any person whose name and address appears on the label pertaining to or attached to a lot or container of agricultural or vegetable seed … for sale and distribution within the state" (§52254.5). Gnome registers as a seller regardless of labeler status. Note "'Labeling' … includes invoices" (§52255) — packing-slip content must be accurate. [EXPLICIT]
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** Registration $40 per fiscal year (July 1–June 30) (§52352). Assessment: statutory cap $0.40/$100; the Secretary's current rate is "$0.30 (thirty cents) for every $100 … of gross annual sales of agricultural, grass or vegetable seed in California for the preceding fiscal year" (application instructions). **Assessment exemptions (§52354):** (a) seed on which another labeler/person already paid (unchanged lot identity); **(b) "No assessment shall be paid on that portion of a person's sales of agricultural or vegetable seed, or both, that is sold in containers of four ounces or less net weight of seed."**; (c) seed sold and shipped out of California. [EXPLICIT] Ordinary retail packets are ≤4 oz net seed weight, so Gnome's expected assessment is $0 — but the registration and the sales declaration are still required.
- **Annual reporting required?** Yes — the annual renewal requires reporting prior-fiscal-year gross California seed sales (lawn/field/vegetable lines on form 68-004), with exemption boxes to claim the ≤4 oz exclusion; records "subject to audit by the California Department of Food and Agriculture." Late: registration penalty 20% / $8, assessment penalty 10% after July 31. [EXPLICIT]
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** No registration exemption for resellers of pre-labeled packets. The double-assessment protection (§52354(a)) and the ≤4 oz assessment exclusion (§52354(b)) are financial, not registration, exemptions. [EXPLICIT]
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** The §52351 registration is it; the application expressly accommodates out-of-state mailing addresses.
- **Small-packet rules:** ≤4 oz containers escape the assessment (§52354(b)); vegetable-seed containers >½ lb need lot number + germination test date on the label (§52453(d)); lots of more than five containers sold to one consumer lose the small-lot labeling relaxation (§52454). Germination-test validity extended for hermetically sealed containers per CCR §3864. [EXPLICIT]
- **Flower-seed coverage:** **Not covered.** "The labeling and sale of flower seed is NOT covered by the California Seed Law." (CDFA application instructions) [EXPLICIT]; consistent with the statutory definitions (agricultural seed "does not … include any variety that is generally known and sold as flower seed or vegetable seed"). Flower-only sales require no registration; vegetable/herb packets trigger it.
- **Destination restrictions:** None found for ordinary home-garden packets at the state level. County-level commercial planting quarantines (e.g., lettuce mosaic programs) target commercial production, not consumer packets. [INTERPRETATION]
- **Noxious-weed concerns:** Contaminant-based noxious weed seed rules (§52257 et seq.); no common vegetable/herb kinds restricted; screen wildflower mixes (unregulated as "flower seed" for registration, but false labeling rules still apply). [INTERPRETATION]
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** The registration text sweeps in any seller "in this state" and the CDFA form explicitly handles out-of-state registrants; enforcement runs through county agricultural commissioners. [EXPLICIT]
- **Product/inventory registration?** None (company-level authorization only).
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio (per current evidence)?** Yes, after registering with CDFA ($40/FY, renew by July 31) and filing the annual gross-sales declaration; expected assessment $0 while all packets are ≤4 oz net seed. Flower-seed-only shipments are outside the law entirely.
