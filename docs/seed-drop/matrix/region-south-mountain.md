## ARKANSAS

- **State:** Arkansas
- **Responsible seed-control agency (exact office):** Arkansas Department of Agriculture, Plant Industries Division — Seed Section (functions of the former State Plant Board; rules still issued as State Plant Board regulations).
- **Official source(s):**
  - Circular 10, "Regulations on the Sale of Planting Seed in Arkansas" (State Plant Board rule, official filed copy, Arkansas Register): https://www.sos.arkansas.gov/uploads/rulesRegs/Arkansas%20Register/2014/0414/209.02.14-001.pdf (accessed 2026-08-13; 2014 filed version — currency caveat in Notes)
  - Agency seed licensing page: https://agriculture.arkansas.gov/crops-industry/quality-control-and-compliance/seed-section/licensing/ (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - Statutory authority cited in Circular 10: Ark. Code Ann. §§ 2-16-207 and 2-16-209. NOTE: the official Arkansas Code (Title 2, incl. Ch. 18) is hosted through arkleg.state.ar.us via LexisNexis and was NOT retrievable for direct reading — see Notes.
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?:** For Gnome's products, likely NO. Circular 10 § 2.A (EXPLICIT): "Any person who sells, offers for sale, or distributes agricultural seed for planting purposes within the state, or who sells or distributes agricultural seed into the state for planting purposes, shall have in force a valid license for such purpose from the State Plant Board." License trigger is **agricultural seed** only. Circular 10 § 1.B (EXPLICIT): "The term 'agricultural seed' includes the seeds of grass, forage, cereal, and fiber crops and any other kinds of seeds commonly recognized within this state as agricultural seeds..." INTERPRETATION: retail packets of vegetable/herb/flower seed are not "agricultural seed," so the Seed Dealer/Labeler's license ($250/yr) does not attach. The agency licensing page (accessed 2026-08-13) reinforces this scope: garden/lima beans, spinach, mustard, turnip, and garden pea seed count as agricultural seed only "when in containers of 25 pounds or more."
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?:** NO — Gnome does not label; supplier labels controlling. Same agricultural-seed scope as above (INTERPRETATION).
- **Inspection or assessment fee (amount/basis):** Tonnage fee $0.10 per hundred pounds applies to **agricultural seed** first sales (agency page; Circular 10 § 2.B). INTERPRETATION: N/A to Gnome's packets.
- **Annual reporting required?:** NO for Gnome (quarterly tonnage reports attach to agricultural-seed licensees only). INTERPRETATION.
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet or labeled-by-another exemption?:** Circular 10 § 2.A Exception 3 (EXPLICIT): "A license is not required for a person who offers for sale, sells, or distributes only seed obtained from a licensed seedsman which is in full compliance with these regulations and on which the tonnage fee is paid by a licensed seedsman." (Belt-and-suspenders route if any product were deemed agricultural seed.)
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?:** Only the agricultural-seed license above ("sells or distributes agricultural seed into the state") — N/A to Gnome's kinds (INTERPRETATION).
- **Small-packet rules:** No packet-specific licensing; vegetable kinds become "agricultural" only at ≥25-lb containers (agency page). Labeling of packets remains subject to the labeling rules — satisfied by intact supplier labels (INTERPRETATION).
- **Flower-seed coverage:** Not within the agricultural-seed licensing scope; not addressed as a licensed category in Circular 10 (EXPLICIT absence).
- **Destination restrictions for ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets:** None found for ordinary garden kinds.
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** Circular 10 prohibited/restricted noxious weed lists apply to seed lots; ordinary cultivated garden kinds are not listed. Supplier-tested, FSA-compliant packets present negligible risk (INTERPRETATION).
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?:** No special regime; "into the state" sellers of agricultural seed are treated the same as in-state (EXPLICIT for ag seed); nothing specific to subscription/DTC.
- **Product/inventory registration?:** None.
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio?:** Likely yes, without any Arkansas license — pending confirmation below.

- **Product/inventory registration?:** None.

## OKLAHOMA

- **State:** Oklahoma
- **Responsible seed-control agency (exact office):** Oklahoma Department of Agriculture, Food, and Forestry (ODAFF), Consumer Protection Services Division — Seed Program.
- **Official source(s):**
  - Oklahoma Seed Law and Rules booklet (2 O.S. §§ 2-8-21 through 2-8-33 + OAC 35:30-25), ODAFF official PDF: https://ag.ok.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/ODAFF-Seed-Law-and-Rules-2023.pdf (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - ODAFF Application for Oklahoma Seed Dealers License: https://ag.ok.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Printable-Seed-License-Dec-2024.pdf (accessed 2026-08-13)
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?:** YES — REGISTRATION REQUIRED. 2 O.S. § 2-8-23(A)(1) (EXPLICIT): "It shall be unlawful for any person to sell, offer for sale, or expose for sale any agricultural seed or vegetable seed within this state: 1. Unless a license has been obtained in accordance with the provisions of Sections 8-21 through 8-29 of this title." And § 2-8-26(6) (EXPLICIT): "Out-of-state wholesale and retail seed dealers who sell or ship agricultural or vegetable seed into this state shall obtain a license in the same manner. A separate license shall be required for each place of business." Application form (EXPLICIT): "Out-of-state firms or individuals are required to obtain a license." Gnome sells vegetable seed to retail purchasers → Retail seed dealer license, $25.00/yr, expires June 30 (OAC 35:30-25-15(a), EXPLICIT: "The annual license fee shall be Twenty Five Dollars ($25.00) for each retail seed dealer... Each license shall expire on June 30 of each year.").
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?:** NO separate labeler license exists; labeling obligations fall on the labeler. Gnome does not label (supplier labels controlling).
- **Inspection or assessment fee (amount/basis):** 8 cents per hundred-pound weight, paid by "the person or vendor responsible for labeling and distributing the seed" (2 O.S. § 2-8-26(8), EXPLICIT). OAC 35:30-25-15(d) (EXPLICIT): "A semi-annual inspection fee of eight cents ($0.08) per hundred pounds shall be paid by every person responsible for labeling and distributing seed to a retail seed licensee in Oklahoma, or each retail seed licensee who processes and sells seed to the consumer on which the inspection fee has not been paid. (1) The minimum semi-annual inspection fee shall be Ten Dollars ($10.00)." Application form (EXPLICIT): "Firms or individuals who do not label seed must have an affidavit on file with the department stating that they sell only seed which has been labeled by another firm or individual who has paid the inspections fees." INTERPRETATION: because Gnome's national suppliers will typically NOT have paid Oklahoma inspection fees on packets Gnome resells, plan for the affidavit + semiannual filing and (worst case) the $10 minimum fee each period; total seed weight is trivial (packets in grams), so 8¢/cwt is de minimis.
- **Annual reporting required?:** YES — semiannual affidavit/report (by last day of January and July for the preceding six months) plus annual license renewal by June 30. Late filing penalty: 10% of amount due or $10, whichever is greater (2 O.S. § 2-8-26(9), EXPLICIT).
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet or labeled-by-another exemption?:** No exemption from the LICENSE. The labeled-by-another concept only shifts the inspection fee (affidavit route above). § 2-8-25 exceptions cover farmer-to-processor sales and carriers only (EXPLICIT).
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?:** YES — out-of-state dealers expressly must license (§ 2-8-26(6), EXPLICIT).
- **Small-packet rules:** None — no small-packet exemption in the OK seed law or rules (EXPLICIT absence; reviewed full booklet).
- **Flower-seed coverage:** NOT covered — the law reaches "agricultural seed or vegetable seed" only; definitions (§ 2-8-21) contain no flower seed category (EXPLICIT absence). Herb seed: covered only if it is "vegetable seeds" ("seeds of those crops which are grown in gardens and on truck farms and are generally known and sold under the name of vegetable seeds in this state") — likely yes for culinary herbs (INTERPRETATION). The vegetable packets alone trigger the license regardless.
- **Destination restrictions for ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets:** None for ordinary kinds.
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** Prohibited/restricted noxious weed lists apply to seed lots (OAC 35:30-25); ordinary garden kinds are not listed; supplier-tested packets fine (INTERPRETATION).
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?:** Licensed "in the same manner" as in-state (EXPLICIT). "Separate license for each place of business" — Gnome has one place of business (Ohio) → one $25 license (INTERPRETATION).
- **Product/inventory registration?:** None (medical-marijuana seed category N/A).
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio?:** YES, once licensed — obtain the $25 Retail seed dealer license before first Oklahoma sale, keep the non-labeler affidavit on file, and calendar the semiannual filings.
- **STATUS:** REGISTRATION REQUIRED
- **Notes:** Lowest-friction registration in the region ($25/yr). One judgment call to confirm at licensing time: whether ODAFF views a DTC subscription shipper as "Retail" ($25) vs "Wholesale/Retail" ($100) — Gnome sells only to consumers, which matches the form's Retail definition ("sell seed to retail purchasers," EXPLICIT).

- **Precise unresolved question(s):** None blocking (license path is explicit). At application: confirm Retail category and whether the affidavit fully substitutes for the 8¢/cwt fee when upstream labelers have not paid Oklahoma fees.

## TEXAS

- **State:** Texas
- **Responsible seed-control agency (exact office):** Texas Department of Agriculture (TDA) — Seed Quality Office (Seed Law program), Giddings, TX.

- **Reseller/dealer registration required?:** CONDITIONALLY. Tex. Agric. Code § 61.013(a) (EXPLICIT): "A person may not sell or offer, expose, or otherwise distribute for sale vegetable seed for planting purposes in this state unless the person possesses a valid vegetable seed license issued under this section." BUT § 61.013(e) (EXPLICIT): "A person who sells or offers, exposes, or otherwise distributes for sale vegetable seed in containers bearing the name and address of a licensee under this section is not required to be licensed under this section." TDA's rule as quoted on Form S297 (EXPLICIT): "a Vegetable Seed License must be obtained by each firm whose name and address appears on the label of the seed" (4 TAC § 9.3(c)). INTERPRETATION: Gnome's packets bear the SUPPLIER's name/address; if each supplier holds a current TX vegetable seed license, Gnome is exempt under § 61.013(e). If any supplier is unlicensed in Texas, either that supplier licenses ($350/yr) or Gnome does.
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?:** The vegetable seed license IS the labeler-anchored license (rule attaches it to the firm on the label). Gnome's name is not on the seed label — under the rule the obligation points at suppliers (INTERPRETATION).
- **Inspection or assessment fee (amount/basis):** Vegetable seed license fee $350.00/yr (Form S297, EXPLICIT). The agricultural-seed inspection fee / Texas Tested Seed Label / quarterly reporting regime (§ 61.011) applies to **agricultural seed** only — grass, forage, cereal, fiber (§ 61.001(1), EXPLICIT) — N/A to Gnome (INTERPRETATION).
- **Annual reporting required?:** NO for vegetable seed (license renews annually; quarterly sworn reports attach only to § 61.011(c) agricultural-seed permit holders — EXPLICIT).
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet or labeled-by-another exemption?:** YES — § 61.013(e), quoted above, but ONLY where the container bears the name/address of a TEXAS licensee (EXPLICIT). This is the state's whole crux for Gnome.
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?:** No separate out-of-state registration; § 61.013 applies to selling "in this state" (INTERPRETATION: DTC ship-in counts as distribution for sale in Texas — assume covered).
- **Small-packet rules:** Labeling for containers under one pound is simplified (§ 61.005(c), EXPLICIT); no small-packet LICENSE exemption.
- **Flower-seed coverage:** NOT covered — Ch. 61 reaches agricultural and vegetable seed only; "vegetable seed" = "seed of any crop that is grown in a garden or on a truck farm and is generally known and sold in this state under the name of vegetable seed" (§ 61.001(2), EXPLICIT). Flower packets are outside the chapter (INTERPRETATION). Herb seed: likely treated as vegetable seed if sold as a garden crop (INTERPRETATION — include in inquiry).
- **Destination restrictions for ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets:** None for ordinary kinds. § 61.019 preempts local seed ordinances (EXPLICIT).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** Noxious weed content limits per § 61.008 and TDA rules apply to seed lots; ordinary garden kinds unaffected; supplier-tested packets fine (INTERPRETATION).
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?:** No — same license/exemption logic; § 61.011(e) shows the law contemplates out-of-state permittees (records/audit), EXPLICIT.
- **Product/inventory registration?:** None.
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio?:** YES if every vegetable/herb packet shipped to Texas bears the name/address of a current TX vegetable seed licensee; otherwise Gnome needs the $350/yr license first.

- **Notes:** Practical path: (1) get TDA's licensee list or per-supplier confirmation; major national packet brands typically hold TX licenses; (2) if any supplier is unlicensed, decide supplier-swap vs Gnome licensing ($350/yr, Form S297 to Seed Quality Office, Giddings).
