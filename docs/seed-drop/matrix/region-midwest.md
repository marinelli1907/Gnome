## Scope and business model (locked)

Boone Systems LLC, an **Ohio** company, resells **unopened, unaltered retail seed
packets** (ordinary vegetable, herb, and flower seed) purchased wholesale from
established domestic suppliers. The supplier's original label stays intact and
controlling. Several packets are combined into a plain padded envelope/box with a
Gnome packing slip, sold as a personalized subscription, and shipped **from Ohio
direct to consumers** in the destination state. No live plants, bulbs, soil,
imported seed, hemp/cannabis, noxious weeds, or quarantined/recalled products.
Boone Systems has **no in-state location** in any state below except Ohio.

**Question answered per state:** may Boone Systems (an out-of-state reseller —
in-state for Ohio) lawfully ship these packets to consumers in the state, and what
licenses/permits/fees/reports does the state seed law impose on it?

- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** No separate "seed dealer" registration exists in ch. 907; the only permit is the **seed labeler permit** (ORC 907.13). EXPLICIT (chapter index contains no dealer-license section; 907.13 is the sole permit provision).
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** The permit attaches to **labeling**, not retail selling: "No person shall label agricultural, vegetable, or flower seed that is intended for sale in this state unless the person holds a valid seed labeler permit." (ORC 907.13, accessed 2026-08-13). EXPLICIT that labeling triggers the permit. Whether ODA treats a subscription reseller of supplier-labeled packets as a person who "labels" seed (Gnome adds only a packing slip) is **not answered by the text** — INTERPRETATION: Gnome does not label and therefore does not need the permit for destination-side selling, but ORC 907.01 defines no "labeler," so agency confirmation is required. Note 907.01 defines "label" as "a tag or other device that is attached to or written, stamped, or printed on any container of seed" — a packing slip accompanying a shipment arguably falls outside this. INTERPRETATION.
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** $50 annual seed labeler permit (expires January 31) plus seed fees reported under ORC 907.14 — vegetable/flower seed in small containers assessed at 2% of wholesale/retail value, credited against the $50 permit minimum (fees under $50 → no additional payment). Fees are owed by **permit holders**; if Gnome needs no permit, it owes no fee (the supplier-labeler does). INTERPRETATION pending confirmation.
- **Annual reporting required?** Only for permit holders: "A person who holds a valid seed labeler permit issued under section 907.13" must report seed sold within Ohio "prior to the first day of February of each year" (ORC 907.14, accessed 2026-08-13). EXPLICIT that the duty attaches to permit holders.
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** No express exemption. ORC 907.09 exceptions cover seed not for sowing, seed in transit to processors, interstate transport under 7 C.F.R. 201.33, noncommercial seed sharing/seed libraries, and bulk export — **nothing for retail resellers of pre-labeled packets** (accessed 2026-08-13). EXPLICIT (absence). The practical exemption is structural: the permit obligation sits on whoever labels.
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** None found in ch. 907. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Small-packet rules:** No container-size carve-out in 907.07; the 2% value-based fee (rather than per-cwt) applies to vegetable/flower seed in small containers under 907.14. Germination-test currency applies at every size.
- **Flower-seed coverage:** Fully covered — 907.13/907.07 expressly reach "agricultural, vegetable, or flower seed"; "vegetable seed" is defined to include seed "sold in this state under the name of vegetable seed or herb seed" (ORC 907.01), so **herb packets ride the vegetable-seed rules**. EXPLICIT.
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None found for ordinary garden seed; no Ohio quarantine reaches sealed retail packets of common garden kinds. INTERPRETATION (absence of any such provision in ch. 907).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** Labels must comply with ORC 907.03 noxious-weed-seed declarations — the supplier-labeler's obligation; ordinary vegetable/herb/flower kinds are not themselves on Ohio's prohibited/restricted noxious lists. INTERPRETATION.
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** N/A for Ohio (Gnome is in-state). ORC 907.01 defines "sell" broadly ("transferring ownership, offering or exposing for sale, exchanging, distributing, giving away, or transporting in this state"), so Gnome's Ohio sales are squarely "sales in this state." EXPLICIT.
- **Product/inventory registration?** None — no per-variety or per-product registration in ch. 907. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio (to Ohio customers)?** Yes, provided every packet carries a compliant supplier label (ORC 907.03) with a **germination test within 12 months of sale** (15 months for cool-season grass, which Gnome does not carry): "No person shall sell any agricultural, vegetable, or flower seed" outside those conditions (ORC 907.07). EXPLICIT that test-date currency binds Gnome as a seller regardless of who labeled. The packet-acceptance checklist (08) must enforce test-date freshness.

## Indiana

- **State:** Indiana
- **Responsible seed-control agency:** Office of Indiana State Chemist & Seed Commissioner (OISC), Purdue University. IC 15-15-1-26: "The state chemist is the state seed commissioner by virtue of office." EXPLICIT (full-chapter text; agency confirmed at https://oisc.purdue.edu/seed/index.html, accessed 2026-08-13).
- **Official source(s):**
  - Statute: IC 15-15-1 (Indiana Seed Law). Official host https://iga.in.gov/laws/current/ic/titles/15#15-15-1 (accessed 2026-08-13 — page loads but serves **no text** to automated fetch; JavaScript-only app). Full verbatim chapter text obtained from Justia's chapter PDF (2014 edition, https://statecodesfiles.justia.com/indiana/2014/title-15/article-15/chapter-1/chapter-1.pdf, accessed 2026-08-13; unofficial host — amendment lines end at P.L.75-2010/P.L.17-2014) and cross-checked against current Justia section captions and official OISC forms, which restate the fee rules.
  - Admin rule: 360 IAC 1-1 (Standards for Seed and Seed Labeling), esp. 360 IAC 1-1-13 (inspection fees) — cited on official OISC Form S-2. Official host https://iar.iga.in.gov/code/current/360/1 (accessed 2026-08-13 — blank to fetch; rule text verified only as restated on official OISC forms).
  - Agency pages: https://oisc.purdue.edu/seed/index.html; forms https://www.oisc.purdue.edu/seed/forms.html; permit application https://www.oisc.purdue.edu/seed/pdf/app_in_seed_permit_2021.pdf; Form S-2 https://www.oisc.purdue.edu/seed/pdf/form_s2.pdf (all accessed 2026-08-13).
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** YES — an "Indiana Seed Permit" (distribution permit). IC 15-15-1-15.5: "'permit' means a written authorization granted by the seed commissioner and required under this chapter before a person may distribute agricultural or vegetable seed in Indiana." EXPLICIT. "Distribute" = "to sell, offer to sell, barter, or supply or transport for sale agricultural or vegetable seed for sowing and seeding purposes in Indiana" (IC 15-15-1-6). EXPLICIT. Shipping to Indiana consumers = distributing in Indiana — INTERPRETATION (strong). The permit application itself carries **no fee**.
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** The permit is framed around distribution plus reporting, not labeling as such — IC 15-15-1-34(b): a distributor "may apply... for a permit to use the person's own labeling, report the quantity of seed sold, and pay the inspection fee on the basis of the report." EXPLICIT. Gnome would NOT be a labeler (supplier's label controls), but Gnome IS a distributor, and per 15.5 the permit attaches to distribution — INTERPRETATION (the 34(b) "may apply" vs. 15.5 "required" tension is an agency question).
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** From official Form S-2 (citing IC 15-15-1-34 and 360 IAC 1-1-13), EXPLICIT: "VEGETABLE SEED PACKETS... IN CONTAINERS NOT MORE THAN ONE POUND. $3.00 per each retail location offered."; packages 1–25 lb: 7.5¢ each (alfalfas/clovers/vegetables/grasses/mixtures) or 3.75¢ (others); bulk/>25 lb: 30¢/cwt or 15¢/cwt; "$10.00 minimum per semi-annual reporting period"; late penalty "10% or $50.00, whichever is greater." Separately, IC 15-15-1-34(a): "A distributor of agricultural seed in packages of not more than one (1) pound shall pay... an annual fee of forty-five dollars ($45) for each brand" — agricultural (incl. lawn) seed only; does NOT apply to vegetable packets. EXPLICIT.
- **Annual reporting required?** YES — semi-annual, under oath, on Form S-2. IC 15-15-1-34(e): report due "semiannually on the last day of the month following the end of the semiannual period" (periods end June 30 / Dec 31). EXPLICIT. Form S-2 shows due dates July 31 / Jan 31.
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** Partial only. IC 15-15-1-34(a): "Payment of an annual fee is not required of retailers who furnish quantities of not more than one (1) pound from a properly labeled container of seed on which the inspection fee has been paid" — EXPLICIT, but this sentence governs the $45 agricultural-brand fee. Form S-2 allows "Fee Paid to Supplier" exemption lines only for packages >1 lb and bulk — NOT for the packet fee, which is per retail location (EXPLICIT on form). Also IC 15-15-1-31(b): a reseller is not penalized for mislabeled seed it could not detect if it obtained "an invoice, genuine grower's declaration, or other labeling information" and took reasonable precautions. EXPLICIT.
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** No separate interstate registration; the ordinary permit + reporting scheme is what reaches out-of-state distributors. INTERPRETATION.
- **Small-packet rules:** IC 15-15-1-33(a) sets packet labeling (≤1 lb): kind and variety; "name and address of the person who labeled or who distributed the seed"; lot; germination info in one of three forms, with sell-by/packed-for dates not exceeding 12 months from the germination test. EXPLICIT. IC 15-15-1-40(1): may not distribute if the germination test is older than 12 months. EXPLICIT — watch inventory freshness; supplier "packed for" dates control.
- **Flower-seed coverage:** NOT covered. The chapter regulates only "agricultural seed" (IC 15-15-1-2) and "vegetable seed" (IC 15-15-1-24); the word "flower" does not appear in the chapter. EXPLICIT (absence, full-chapter text). Herb seed most likely counts as vegetable seed ("crops grown commercially or in home gardens... commonly known and sold in Indiana as vegetable seeds"). INTERPRETATION.
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None found in the seed law. EXPLICIT (absence in chapter; hemp separately permitted and excluded from Gnome's model).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** IC 15-15-1-40(4)–(6): may not distribute seed containing prohibited noxious weed seed, restricted noxious weed seed >0.25%, or >2.5% total weed seed. EXPLICIT. Ordinary cultivated garden kinds are not on the lists; rests on supplier quality (2018 noxious-weed list rule LSA #18-443, https://www.oisc.purdue.edu/seed/pdf/lsa_18-443_rule_FINAL.pdf — located, not fetched).
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** No special treatment in the statute; same permit/report scheme. INTERPRETATION. How "$3.00 per each retail location offered" maps onto a DTC e-commerce seller with zero Indiana retail locations is genuinely unclear — possibly $0 packet fee but still the $10/period minimum. UNVERIFIED — agency question.
- **Product/inventory registration?** None per variety. The per-brand $45 annual fee applies only to agricultural seed in ≤1 lb packages — not Gnome's vegetable/herb/flower packets. EXPLICIT.
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio?** Yes, after obtaining the (free) Indiana Seed Permit and committing to semi-annual Form S-2 reports and inspection fees (likely the $10 minimum per semi-annual period, i.e., ~$20/yr, pending the "retail location" question). Supplier labels satisfy content requirements if kept within the 12-month germination-test window.

Statute text verified from an unofficial full-text PDF (2014) cross-checked against current Justia captions and official OISC forms; the official IGA site would not serve text. Licensed-dealer lookup: https://www.inplants.oisc.purdue.edu/USAPlantsIN/Licenses/External/LicensedSeedDealerSearch.aspx (useful to check whether Gnome's suppliers already hold Indiana permits).

## Illinois

- **State:** Illinois
- **Responsible seed-control agency:** Illinois Department of Agriculture (IDOA), Bureau of Agricultural Products Inspection (issues the "Illinois Agriculture and Vegetable Seed Permits"). EXPLICIT (https://agr.illinois.gov/consumers/inspection.html, accessed 2026-08-13).
- **Official source(s):**
  - Statute: 505 ILCS 110 (Illinois Seed Law), full act at https://www.ilga.gov/Legislation/ILCS/Articles?ActID=1696&ChapterID=40 (accessed 2026-08-13); section pages e.g. https://www.ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/050501100K10.htm (accessed 2026-08-13). Note: ActID is 1696 (legacy ilcs3.asp URLs are dead).
  - Admin rule: 8 Ill. Adm. Code Part 230, full part at https://www.ilga.gov/agencies/JCAR/EntirePart?titlepart=00800230 (accessed 2026-08-13).
  - Agency page: https://agr.illinois.gov/plants/seeds.html (accessed 2026-08-13); permit application https://agr.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/agr/plants/seeds/documents/ag-vegseedpermit.pdf (accessed 2026-08-13).
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** YES, presumptively — a Retail Seed Dealer Permit, $10/year. IDOA (EXPLICIT): Retail Seed Dealer Permit ($10.00) — "Any person who sells agricultural, vegetable or lawn seed to consumers." Rule 230.70 (EXPLICIT): "It is unlawful for any person to sell, offer for sale, expose for sale or transport for sale any seed... within this State without meeting the requirements listed in Section 10 of the Act..." — subject to the exemption below. Whether "in this State" captures an out-of-state shipper turns on 505 ILCS 110/2.112 (EXPLICIT): "'Distribute' means to import, consign, offer for sale, hold for sale, barter, or otherwise supply seed in this State" — importing seed into Illinois is covered. INTERPRETATION (strong).
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** Illinois has no separate "labeler" license; the Section 10 permit reaches persons whose name/address appears on the label or invoice of seed distributed, persons who repackage, and auction sellers (EXPLICIT in substance; section fetched twice with consistent content). Gnome does not label (supplier's label controls), but Gnome's name on its invoice/packing slip plus retail selling makes the retail-dealer category the operative one. INTERPRETATION.
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** Flat annual, set by rule — 8 Ill. Adm. Code 230.70(a), EXPLICIT (quoted from official Part text): "1) Retail seed dealer ... $10.00; 2) Wholesale seed dealer ... $30.00; 3) Seed broker ... $30.00; 4) Seed merchant ... $40.00; 5) Operator(s) of public auction ... $30.00." Confirmed on the official application PDF. Not gross-sales based. Permit year July 1–June 30 (505 ILCS 110/10). EXPLICIT.
- **Annual reporting required?** Permit renews annually. A "Seed Disposition Report" exists (8 Ill. Adm. Code 230.270; 505 ILCS 110/11.2) — it concerns agricultural seed purchased from Illinois producers and disposed of as non-seed, filed with renewal (EXPLICIT as to existence). Almost certainly inapplicable to Gnome's model. INTERPRETATION. No tonnage/gross-sales report found.
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** YES — the key Illinois provision, 505 ILCS 110/10 (EXPLICIT, verbatim, confirmed in two independent fetches): "No permit shall be required of persons selling only the packaged lines which have been packed and distributed by a person, firm or corporation holding and having a permit in force." If Gnome sells only packet lines packed/distributed by suppliers holding current Illinois permits, Gnome needs no permit. Fact-dependent per supplier.
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** None separate; the permit scheme is the only registration. INTERPRETATION.
- **Small-packet rules:** Vegetable seed packet labeling per 505 ILCS 110/4.4 — variety name, germination if below standard with "Below Standard" in 8-point type, labeler's name/address, lot, packing year or test date (EXPLICIT in substance from full-act fetch). Bulk-sale allowance in 230.40(c) is inapplicable (Gnome sells sealed packets).
- **Flower-seed coverage:** NOT covered. The Act defines agricultural seeds (2.102) and vegetable seeds (2.141); it does not define or cover flower seeds (EXPLICIT absence per full-act review; IDOA permit categories reference only "agricultural, vegetable or lawn seed"). Flower-only packets appear outside the Act. INTERPRETATION.
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None in the Seed Law for ordinary garden packets. EXPLICIT (absence). 505 ILCS 110/7(b) even exempts non-commercial seed sharing/libraries — irrelevant to Gnome but shows the Act's consumer-protection focus.
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** 8 Ill. Adm. Code 230.20 (prohibited) and 230.30 (restricted; max "1 to 1000 agricultural seeds"). EXPLICIT. Ordinary cultivated kinds not listed; supplier quality controls. Administrative penalties: $500 (stop-sale noncompliance), $100 (label violations) — 505 ILCS 110/12 (EXPLICIT in substance).
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** No special provisions; treated as any seller distributing into Illinois. INTERPRETATION.
- **Product/inventory registration?** None per variety/product.
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio?** Yes. Either (a) confirm every packet line comes from a supplier "holding and having a permit in force" in Illinois (exemption applies), or (b) simply obtain the $10/year Retail Seed Dealer Permit and moot the question. Flower-only items are outside the Act.
- **STATUS:** REGISTRATION REQUIRED (a $10/yr Retail Seed Dealer Permit unless the supplier-permit exemption is documented for every line; the statute clearly imposes the permit on retail sellers, so this is the safe classification).
