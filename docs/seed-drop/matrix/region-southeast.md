## Scope and business model (locked)

Boone Systems LLC, an **Ohio** company, resells **unopened, unaltered retail seed
packets** (ordinary vegetable, herb, and flower seed) purchased wholesale from
established domestic suppliers. The supplier's original label stays intact and
controlling. Several packets are combined into a plain padded envelope/box with a
Gnome packing slip, sold as a personalized subscription, and shipped **from Ohio
direct to consumers** in the destination state. No live plants, bulbs, soil,
imported seed, hemp/cannabis, noxious weeds, or quarantined/recalled products.
Boone Systems has **no in-state location** in any state below.

**Question answered per state:** may Boone Systems (an out-of-state reseller)
lawfully ship these packets to consumers in the state, and what
licenses/permits/fees/reports does the state seed law impose on it?

## Delaware

- **State:** Delaware
- **Responsible seed-control agency:** Delaware Department of Agriculture (DDA), Plant Industries Section — Seed, Grain & Sod Services
- **Official source(s):**
  - 3 Del. C. ch. 15 (Seeds), §§ 1501–1512 (full chapter): https://delcode.delaware.gov/title3/c015/index.html (accessed 2026-08-13; full chapter text pulled and searched directly)
  - DDA Seed, Grain & Sod Services page: https://agriculture.delaware.gov/plant-industries/seed-grain-services/ (accessed 2026-08-13)
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** **No.** Chapter 15 (the entire Delaware seed law, §§ 1501–1512) contains **no dealer license, seller registration, or labeler permit of any kind**. A direct text search of the full chapter finds "register/permit/license" only in the seed-certification context (certified/registered/foundation seed, § 1508) and lab-fee powers (§ 1507). EXPLICIT (absence, chapter fully enumerated and searched 2026-08-13).
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** No — no such provision exists. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** None on sellers. § 1507(a)(6) lets DDA "fix and collect charges" for germination tests, and § 1507(a)(9) covers inspection/certification fees for the voluntary certification program — neither applies to a reseller. EXPLICIT.
- **Annual reporting required?** No. No reporting provision exists for sellers. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** Not needed (no license exists). The labeling duty is disjunctive and satisfied by the supplier's label: vegetable containers of 1 lb or less must show "The name and address of the person who labeled the seed or who sells, offers, or exposes the seed for sale within this State" (§ 1502B(a)(4); same structure for flower seed under §§ 1502/1502C) (accessed 2026-08-13). EXPLICIT.
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** None. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Small-packet rules:** § 1502B (vegetable, ≤1 lb) and § 1502C (flower packets) prescribe retail-packet label content (kind/variety, packed-for year or germination date, labeler name) — the supplier-labeler's obligation on packets Gnome resells.
- **Flower-seed coverage:** Yes — § 1502C carries dedicated flower-seed labeling and germination-standard rules. EXPLICIT.
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None found in ch. 15 for ordinary garden kinds. INTERPRETATION (absence).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** § 1504(a) makes it unlawful to sell seed containing prohibited noxious weed seeds or mislabeled seed — a seller-side duty satisfied by compliant supplier labels; common vegetable/herb/flower kinds are not themselves noxious in Delaware. INTERPRETATION.
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** No differential treatment anywhere in ch. 15. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Product/inventory registration?** None. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio (per current evidence)?** **Yes.** No license/registration/fee/report attaches to selling; Gnome's duties are the § 1504 seller prohibitions (properly labeled, non-noxious, truthful advertising), which the supplier's intact label satisfies, plus § 1505 record-keeping only for "each person whose name appears on the label as handling" seed — the supplier, not Gnome. EXPLICIT for the absence of licensing; INTERPRETATION that § 1505 does not reach Gnome (Gnome's name is not on the label).
- **STATUS:** CLEARED
- **Notes:** Cleanest state in the region: labeling-only law with no permit scheme. Keep supplier labels intact (§ 1504(b) makes detaching/altering labels unlawful) and honor test-date/packed-for-year currency via the packet-acceptance checklist (08). Delaware's general business-license regime (Title 30) is outside seed-law scope and not evaluated here.
- **Date verified:** 2026-08-13
- **Precise unresolved question(s):** None material under the seed law. (Optional courtesy note to DDA is drafted in the outreach backlog but not required.)
- **VERIFIED agency contact:** Seed program — Olivia Everett, Environmental Scientist III / Seed Certification Specialist, olivia.everett@delaware.gov, 302-698-4596; Delaware Department of Agriculture, 2320 S. DuPont Hwy, Dover, DE 19901. Verified on the DDA Seed, Grain & Sod Services page 2026-08-13. (No seed-law-enforcement-specific inbox is published; the named contact is the certification specialist — routing piece unverified.)

## Maryland

- **State:** Maryland
- **Responsible seed-control agency:** Maryland Department of Agriculture (MDA), Turf and Seed Section
- **Official source(s):**
  - Md. Code, Agriculture § 9-201 (definitions): https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gag&section=9-201&enactments=false (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - § 9-204 (wholesale seedsman's permit): https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gag&section=9-204&enactments=false (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - § 9-208 (vegetable/herb labeling), § 9-210 (prohibited acts), § 9-211 (exemptions), §§ 9-202/9-203/9-205/9-206 (secretary duties, stop-sale, records, treated seed) — same StatuteText URL pattern, sections 9-208, 9-210, 9-211, 9-202, 9-203, 9-206 (all accessed 2026-08-13)
  - MDA Turf and Seed page: https://mda.maryland.gov/plants-pests/Pages/turf_seed.aspx (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - MDA seedsman's permit application (fee/remit address): https://mda.maryland.gov/plants-pests/Documents/tns_seedsman_app.pdf (accessed 2026-08-13)
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** **No — the only permit in Subtitle 2 is for wholesale seedsmen.** § 9-204(a): "No person may engage in the business of a wholesale seedsman in the State unless he first obtains a permit." § 9-201 defines "wholesale seedsman" as one who sells seed "to a retail seedsman" and "retail seedsman" as one who sells "to the consumer." Gnome sells only to consumers → Gnome is a retail seedsman, and **no retail seedsman permit exists anywhere in Subtitle 2** (all sections enumerated 2026-08-13). EXPLICIT.
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** No labeler permit exists in Subtitle 2. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** None on retail sales. The $100/year wholesale permit fee (§ 9-204(c); permit year starts July 1) does not apply. EXPLICIT.
- **Annual reporting required?** No — records/reports duties (§ 9-205) attach to "Each wholesale seedsman or conditioner of seed." EXPLICIT.
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** Not needed (no retail permit). Labeling is disjunctive and satisfied by the supplier's label: "The name and address of the person who labeled the seed or who sells, offers or exposes the seed for sale in the State" (§ 9-208, containers under 1 lb) (accessed 2026-08-13). EXPLICIT. § 9-211 exemptions (non-planting seed, farmer-to-farmer, carriers) are not needed for this model.
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** Only for out-of-state **wholesale** seedsmen: "Out-of-state wholesale seedsmen doing business in the State shall obtain a permit in the same manner" (§ 9-204(d); MDA page and application use the same language). Gnome does not sell to retail seedsmen → not covered. EXPLICIT.
- **Small-packet rules:** § 9-208 sets reduced label content for containers under 1 lb (kind/variety, packed-for year or germination date, labeler/seller name).
- **Flower-seed coverage:** Yes — § 9-209 covers flower/tree/shrub seed labeling; § 9-210 prohibitions reach "agricultural, vegetable, herb, flower, tree, or shrub seed." Herb seed is expressly named. EXPLICIT.
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None found for ordinary garden kinds. INTERPRETATION (absence).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** § 9-210(a)(4)-(6) bars selling seed containing prohibited noxious weed seeds or >2.50% weed seed — supplier-label compliance issue; common garden kinds are not Maryland noxious plants. INTERPRETATION.
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** Only via § 9-204(d), which reaches wholesale only. No provision reaches an out-of-state retail (direct-to-consumer) seller. EXPLICIT.
- **Product/inventory registration?** None in Subtitle 2. EXPLICIT (absence). (Maryland's separate Turf Grass Law, Subtitle 1, regulates turf grass seed mixtures — Gnome sells none.)
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio (per current evidence)?** **Yes.** No permit, fee, or report attaches to retail DTC sales; Gnome's duties are the § 9-210 seller prohibitions (proper labels, germination-test currency of 9 months unless packets carry the packed-for-year form permitted for sub-1-lb containers under § 9-208), satisfied by compliant supplier labels. EXPLICIT for permit absence; INTERPRETATION on label-currency mechanics.
- **STATUS:** CLEARED
- **Notes:** The wholesale/retail split is the whole analysis: the permit follows sales **to retail seedsmen**, and Gnome makes none. If Gnome ever wholesales packets to a store or another reseller in Maryland, the $100 wholesale seedsman permit (July 1 year; remit P.O. Box 17304, Baltimore, MD 21297-1304) becomes mandatory before first sale.
- **Date verified:** 2026-08-13
- **Precise unresolved question(s):** None material. (Watch item, not a legal question: MDA's OneStop portal titles the form "Seedsman Permit" without the word "wholesale" — the statute and the application body both limit it to wholesale seedsmen.)
- **VERIFIED agency contact:** Jim Drews, Turf & Seed Program Manager, MDA, jim.drews@maryland.gov, (410) 841-5960; 50 Harry S. Truman Parkway, Annapolis, MD 21401. Verified on the MDA Turf and Seed page 2026-08-13.

## West Virginia

- **State:** West Virginia
- **Responsible seed-control agency:** West Virginia Department of Agriculture (WVDA), Regulatory & Environmental Affairs Division — Agricultural Materials (Seedsman registration)
- **Official source(s):**
  - W. Va. Code § 19-16-3 (certificate of registration; seed fees): https://code.wvlegislature.gov/19-16-3/ (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - W. Va. Code § 19-16-1 (definitions): https://code.wvlegislature.gov/19-16-1/ (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - WVDA Agricultural Materials page (seedsman registration, tonnage fees, forms): https://agriculture.wv.gov/divisions/regulatory-and-environmental-affairs/agricultural-materials/ (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - W. Va. Code R. 61-9 (seed rule; sets the application fee) — **not directly accessed** 2026-08-13; fee amount unverified
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** **Yes (with a statutory escape hatch).** "No person may distribute any agricultural, vegetable, tree and shrub, or flower seeds, or seed potatoes without a valid certificate of registration" (§ 19-16-3). "Distribute" means "to offer for sale, sell, expose for sale, exchange, or barter seed for sowing purposes within the state" (§ 19-16-1). WVDA: "Any persons distributing agricultural, vegetable, tree and shrub, or flower seeds, or seed potatoes must be registered as a Seedsman." EXPLICIT for the registration duty; INTERPRETATION that shipping DTC into WV is distribution "within the state."
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** No separate labeler permit — registration is the single instrument. EXPLICIT.
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** Application fee "set forth in a legislative rule" (§ 19-16-3; amount in Rule 61-9 — unknown, see unresolved). Tonnage fee **$0.50 per 100 lbs** of seed distributed in the state (WVDA page). At packet scale this is pennies per drop but must still be reported. EXPLICIT (rate), unknown (application fee).
- **Annual reporting required?** **Quarterly** poundage reports for registration holders, periods ending March 31, June 30, September 30, December 31 (§ 19-16-3 / WVDA page: reports due Jan 31, Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31). Registration expires December 31; re-apply ≥15 days before expiry or before starting business. EXPLICIT.
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** **Yes — the strongest in the region:** "A dealer may not be required to register, if he or she can prove that the person he or she is obtaining the seed from has a valid certificate of registration" (§ 19-16-3). "Dealer" = "any person who exclusively sells … seed for sowing purposes within this state to the ultimate consumer" (§ 19-16-1) — Gnome's exact model. If Gnome's suppliers hold WV registrations, Gnome can claim the exemption. EXPLICIT. Separately, vegetable packets ≤8 oz sold from display units can use $10 **seed stamps** per display in lieu of tonnage reporting — a retail-store program that does not map to mail order. EXPLICIT (existence), INTERPRETATION (inapplicability).
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** § 19-16-2(t) requires "the full name and address of the interstate shipper" on labels of interstate shipments — supplier-label matter. No separate interstate registration beyond the certificate above. EXPLICIT.
- **Small-packet rules:** ≤8 oz vegetable-packet seed-stamp option (above); labeling per § 19-16-2.
- **Flower-seed coverage:** Yes — "flower seeds" defined (§ 19-16-1, including wildflower) and covered by § 19-16-3. Herb seed rides the "vegetable or herb seeds" definition. EXPLICIT.
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None found for ordinary garden kinds. INTERPRETATION (absence).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** Standard label/noxious prohibitions on the labeled product; common garden kinds not restricted. INTERPRETATION.
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** Not expressly; the registration/dealer-exemption framework applies by activity, not location. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Product/inventory registration?** None (no per-product registration). EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio (per current evidence)?** Yes after either (a) registering as a Seedsman (application fee per Rule 61-9; quarterly poundage reports; $0.50/cwt tonnage) or (b) documenting that every supplier of packets shipped to WV holds a valid WV certificate of registration (§ 19-16-3 dealer exemption). Path (a) is the low-risk default; path (b) needs supplier verification workflow in the supplier-review doc (07).
- **STATUS:** REGISTRATION REQUIRED
- **Notes:** Cheap but reporting-heavy (quarterly, even at packet volumes). Ask WVDA in the same message for the current Rule 61-9 application fee and whether the dealer exemption requires advance filing or is self-executing proof-on-request.

  1. Current Seedsman registration application fee under Rule 61-9 (not published on the WVDA page).
  2. Is the § 19-16-3 dealer exemption self-executing (proof on request) or does WVDA require a filing naming the registered suppliers?
  3. Does WVDA apply "distribute … within the state" to an out-of-state mail-order seller with no WV presence?

## North Carolina

- **State:** North Carolina
- **Responsible seed-control agency:** North Carolina Department of Agriculture & Consumer Services (NCDA&CS), Plant Industry Division — Seed & Fertilizer Section
- **Official source(s):**
  - G.S. 106-277.18 (registration/license duty): https://ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_106/gs_106-277.18.html (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - G.S. 106-277.28 (license and inspection fees): https://ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_106/gs_106-277.28.html (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - G.S. 106-277.2 (definitions): https://ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_106/gs_106-277.2.html (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - Art. 31 full text: https://www.ncleg.net/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/ByArticle/Chapter_106/Article_31.html (accessed 2026-08-13)
  - NCDA&CS seed dealer licenses page: https://www.ncagr.gov/divisions/plant-industry/seed-and-fertilizer/seed-dealer-licenses (accessed 2026-08-13)
- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** **Yes.** "It shall be the duty of the Commissioner … to require each seed dealer selling, offering or exposing for sale in, or exporting from, this State any agricultural or vegetable seeds for seeding purposes, including packet or package seeds, to register with the Commissioner and to obtain a license annually" (G.S. 106-277.18, accessed 2026-08-13). "Dealer" = "any person, not classified as a grower, who buys, sells or offers for sale any seed for seeding purposes" (G.S. 106-277.2). Packet seeds are named expressly. EXPLICIT for the duty; INTERPRETATION that a remote DTC seller is "selling … in … this State."
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** No separate labeler permit — the dealer license is the instrument. EXPLICIT.
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** License: **retail seed dealer $30.00/year per location**; wholesale or combined $125.00 (G.S. 106-277.28). Inspection fee: "four cents (4¢) for each container of seeds weighing 10 pounds or more" — retail packets weigh ounces, so **no per-container inspection fee** on Gnome's product. EXPLICIT.
- **Annual reporting required?** License renews by January 1 annually; § 106-277.28 requires dealers to "report quarterly … the quantity and container weight of seeds first offered for sale that quarter" (reports due Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct 1 +10 days; 10% penalty for late fees). Whether NCDA&CS expects quarterly reports from a dealer whose containers are all under 10 lbs (zero fee due) is unresolved. EXPLICIT (duty exists) + unknown (application to packet-only dealers — see unresolved question).
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** None. The registration duty expressly includes "packet or package seeds," and no labeled-by-another exemption exists in Art. 31. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** No separate provision; the 106-277.18 duty (sell in / export from) is the only hook. NCDA&CS's page does not address mail-order sellers. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Small-packet rules:** Packets are inside the license duty but outside the 4¢ inspection fee (<10 lb containers). EXPLICIT.
- **Flower-seed coverage:** **No** — the law covers "agricultural seeds" (incl. lawn) and "vegetable seeds" only; "Neither flower seed nor herb seed appears in these definitions" (G.S. 106-277.2, accessed 2026-08-13). Gnome's vegetable packets alone trigger the license; flower-only shipments would not. Herb seed most plausibly rides the vegetable-seed definition ("crops which are grown in gardens"). EXPLICIT (flower absent); INTERPRETATION (herb within vegetable).
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None found for ordinary garden kinds. INTERPRETATION (absence).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** Art. 31 noxious-weed content limits apply to the labeled product; common garden kinds are not restricted kinds. INTERPRETATION.
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** Not addressed on the statute's face or the NCDA&CS page. The registration form (SF04) plus Tonnage/Seed Reporting Permit form exist for all dealers. EXPLICIT (absence of special rule).
- **Product/inventory registration?** None. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio (per current evidence)?** Yes after obtaining the **retail seed dealer license ($30/year, renew by Jan 1, Form SF04)**, with quarterly reporting mechanics to be confirmed for a packets-only remote dealer. ## Alabama

- **State:** Alabama
- **Responsible seed-control agency:** Alabama Department of Agriculture & Industries (ADAI), Agricultural Compliance Section — Seed
- **Official source(s):**
  - Code of Ala. Title 2 ch. 26 (Seeds) incl. §§ 2-26-5, 2-26-14, 2-26-15, as posted by ADAI: https://agi.alabama.gov/agcompliance/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2020/12/AlabamaSeedLawandRules.pdf (accessed 2026-08-13; full text extracted)

- **Reseller/dealer registration required?** **Yes.** § 2-26-5(a): "Every person who sells, offers for sale, exposes for sale, distributes or solicits orders for the sale of any agricultural, vegetable, herb, tree, shrub, or flower seed to retail seed dealers, farmers, or **to others who use or plant such seed in the State of Alabama** shall, before selling or offering such seed for sale … and on or before January 1 of each year secure an annual permit from the Commissioner of Agriculture and Industries." Selling to Alabama consumers who plant the seed is squarely inside the text. EXPLICIT.
- **Seed-labeler permit/license required?** No separate labeler permit — the annual seed permit is the instrument (processor permits under § 2-26-5(c) don't apply; Gnome does not clean/process/treat). EXPLICIT.
- **Inspection or assessment fee:** Two layers. (1) **Permit fee** per place of business, set by the Board within statutory ranges keyed to Alabama gross receipts: receipts ≤$2,500 → $5–$15, up to $600–$700 for receipts over $500,000 (§ 2-26-5(a)(2); first year uses anticipated receipts). (2) **Seed inspection fee** (§ 2-26-15(c)): "Every person who sells or distributes seed for sale, whether in bulk or in containers, **within the state or into the state** for planting purposes, shall be assessed a seed inspection fee as established by the board," and § 2-26-15(d)(1): payment "shall be the responsibility of the person initiating the first sale of seed within or into the state" — for Ohio→AL DTC shipments that is **Gnome**. Current board-set rates unverified (application PDF unreadable). EXPLICIT (structure/brackets); unknown (current rates).
- **Annual reporting required?** **Yes — quarterly.** § 2-26-15(d)(3): "File quarterly reports … covering the total pounds of all sales of seed subject to the fee … no later than 30 days following the end of each calendar quarter," plus record-keeping under § 2-26-15(d)(2) and § 2-26-10. Permit renews on/before January 1. EXPLICIT.
- **Sealed-original-retail-packet / labeled-by-another exemption?** None usable. § 2-26-14 exemptions cover in-presence consumer sales from a labeled container, non-planting seed, processing storage, and farmer sales — nothing for remote resellers of pre-labeled packets. The § 2-26-5(a)(1) rack-display packet permit (≤8 oz packets on supplemental container displays, fee ≤$7.50/display) is a store-display instrument, not a mail-order one. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Interstate seller/shipper registration?** Same permit; § 2-26-5(a)(3) even waives the first-year permit **fee** for "Out-of-state seed sellers not previously selling seed in Alabama which sell or distribute seed through a representative soliciting orders in Alabama" — written for the traveling-representative model; whether ADAI extends it to e-commerce is unknown (fee waiver only; the permit itself is still required). EXPLICIT (provision exists); unknown (application to DTC).
- **Small-packet rules:** Rack-display permit alternative (above) for ≤8 oz packets on displays; otherwise packets ride the ordinary permit + inspection-fee system. EXPLICIT.
- **Flower-seed coverage:** Yes — "agricultural, vegetable, **herb**, tree, shrub, or flower seed" (§ 2-26-5(a)); herb seed is expressly named. EXPLICIT.
- **Destination restrictions (ordinary vegetable/herb/flower packets):** None found for ordinary garden kinds. INTERPRETATION (absence).
- **Noxious-weed concerns for common garden kinds:** Art. 2 (fungi/noxious-weed elimination, §§ 2-26-30 ff.) targets crop seed/small grains; ordinary garden kinds with compliant supplier labels are unaffected. INTERPRETATION.
- **Out-of-state subscription seller treated differently?** Permit applies identically; § 2-26-15(c) "into the state" language makes the inspection fee reach out-of-state shippers by name. First-sale-into-state responsibility lands on Gnome. EXPLICIT.
- **Product/inventory registration?** None. EXPLICIT (absence).
- **Can Gnome ship directly from Ohio (per current evidence)?** Yes after (1) securing the annual permit before first sale (bracket fee ~$5–$15 at launch volumes, anticipated-receipts basis) and (2) enrolling in quarterly inspection-fee reporting on total pounds shipped into Alabama. ADAI now runs online registration on agi.alabama.gov. EXPLICIT path.
